2. 처리 목적

  • 게임 계정 생성 및 게임 진행 저장·복원(앱을 다시 설치하거나 기기를 바꿨을 때 Google Play 게임즈 또는 Game Center 계정으로 기록 복원)
  • 닉네임 표시, 중복·부적절한 닉네임 방지, 랭킹 운영 및 랭킹 보상(스킨) 지급
  • 유료 상품 결제 확인, 지급, 중복 지급 방지, 환불 및 고객 문의 처리
  • 부정 이용(기록 조작 등) 방지, 오류 분석 및 서비스 개선
  • 보상형 광고 제공, 광고 보상 지급, 광고 성과 측정 및 부정 광고 활동 방지

3. 보유 및 이용 기간

계정을 삭제하거나 처리 목적이 달성되면 지체 없이 파기합니다. 단, 관련 법령에 따라 보존이 필요한 정보는 아래 기간 동안 보관합니다.

  • 대금 결제 및 재화 등의 공급에 관한 기록: 5년 (전자상거래 등에서의 소비자보호에 관한 법률)
  • 소비자 불만 또는 분쟁 처리에 관한 기록: 3년 (같은 법)
  • 서비스 접속 기록: 3개월 (통신비밀보호법)

SNAILIX Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026 · Operator: QUOZ (PARK JUN)

QUOZ (PARK JUN) ("we") operates the mobile game SNAILIX ("the Game"). The Game has no sign-up: each device gets an anonymous account automatically. This policy explains what we process and why.

1. Information we process

  • Account: an anonymous player ID generated on first launch, your Google Play Games player ID when your Android device is signed in to Google Play Games and the game links it to protect your save, and your Apple Game Center player ID when you link Game Center on iPhone.
  • Gameplay: nickname, game progress (stage records, stars, in-game currency, items, skins), endless-mode records and leaderboard scores.
  • Purchases: products bought, purchase time, and the order identifier from the store receipt (stored hashed). Payment card details are handled by Google Play and Apple; we never receive them.
  • Automatically generated: access time, usage statistics (stage start/end, play time, ad results), error and crash logs, device model and OS version.
  • Ads: advertising ID (Android: Google advertising ID; iPhone: IDFA, only if you allow tracking), ad request and view records, IP address and device information (collected by our ad provider, Google AdMob).

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address or postal address.

2. Why we use it

To create your game account, save your progress and restore it after a reinstall or on a new phone (through Google Play Games or Game Center); to show nicknames, prevent duplicate or offensive names, run leaderboards and give ranking rewards; to verify and deliver purchases, prevent double delivery and handle refunds and support; to prevent cheating, fix errors and improve the Game; and to provide rewarded ads, give ad rewards, measure ad performance and prevent invalid ad activity.

3. Retention

We delete your data without delay when you delete your account or when it is no longer needed, except where law requires us to keep it: payment and delivery records for 5 years and complaint records for 3 years (Korean E-Commerce Act), and access logs for 3 months (Protection of Communications Secrets Act).

4. Sharing

We do not sell or provide your personal information to third parties, except where required by law.

5. Service providers and international transfer

  • Unity Technologies (privacy@unity3d.com), United States and other Unity data-center locations: game server hosting (authentication, save data, leaderboards, server logic), usage analytics (Unity Analytics) and crash diagnostics (Unity Diagnostics). All information in section 1 is transferred over the network while you play, and kept until your account is deleted or the service contract ends.
  • Google LLC, United States: Google Play Games sign-in (account link, Play Games player ID); in-app payment processing; rewarded ads via Google AdMob (advertising ID, ad request and view records, IP address, device information). Handled under Google's privacy policy.
  • Apple Inc., United States: Game Center sign-in (account link, Game Center player ID); App Store in-app payment processing. Handled under Apple's privacy policy.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or to stop its processing, at any time. You can delete your account from the account-deletion menu in the Game, or email us with your nickname. Deletion removes all progress, currency and purchased items permanently. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Privacy › Ads); ads are still shown but are no longer personalised. On iPhone you can turn off tracking for the Game in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. We respond without delay (within 10 days).

7. Children

The Game does not collect real-name or contact information. In Korea, children under 14 need a legal guardian's consent to make purchases, and guardians may ask us to view or delete the child's data.

8. Security

Game progress is stored in server-only storage that players cannot modify, every change is validated on the server, receipt order identifiers are stored hashed, server access is limited to the operator, and data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).
